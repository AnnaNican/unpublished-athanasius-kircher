# Bibliography of Athanasius Kircher's Works

| Title | Year | Location | Description |
|-------|------|----------|-------------|
| Ars magnesia | 1631 | Würzburg | His first publication, on magnetism. |
| Prodromus coptus sive Aegyptiacus | 1636 | Rome | His first essay on the Egyptian language. |
| Specula Melitensis encyclica | 1638 | Naples | On an invention for calculating many calendrical and astronomical problems. |
| Scrutinium pestis physico-medicum | 1640 | Graeci | On the causes of the plague, which he attributes to germs on the grounds of his microscopic observations. |
| Magnes, sive de Arte Magnetica | 1641 | Rome | His largest work on magnetism. See our Chapter VII. |
| Lingua Aegyptiaca restituta | 1643 | Rome | Shows that Coptic is a vestige of the language of ancient Egypt. |
| Ars magna lucis et umbrae | 1646 | Rome | On astronomy, optics, etc. See our Chapter VII. |
| Rituale ecclesiae Aegyptiacae sive cophitarum | 1647 | n.p. | Translation of the Coptic liturgy. |
| Musurgia universalis | 1650 | 2 vols., Rome | On music. See our Chapter VI. |
| Obeliscus Pamphilius | 1650 | Rome | On the obelisk now in the Piazza Navona, Rome. |
| Oedipus Aegyptiacus | 1652 | 3 vols., Rome | His major work on philosophy and Egyptology. See our Chapter V. |
| Itinerarium exstaticum | 1656 | Rome | His astronomical journey. |
| Iter exstaticum II | 1657 | Rome | An underground journey, prologue to Mundus subterraneus. |
| Diatribe de prodigiosis Crucibus | 1661 | Rome | On the crosses seen imprinted on garments after an eruption of Vesuvius. |
| Polygraphia nova | 1663 | Rome | Suggestion for a universal language of symbols, with vocabulary in Latin, Italian, Spanish, French and German. |
| Arithmologia | 1665 | Rome | On the true and false meanings of number. |
| Historia Eustachio – Mariana | 1665 | Rome | On the sanctuary he discovered at Mentorella. |
| Mundus subterraneus | 1665 | 2 vols., Amsterdam | On geology and many other subterranean topics; probably his most popular work. See our Chapter VIII. |
| Obeliscus aegyptiacus | 1666 | Rome | On a newly unearthed obelisk. See our Chapter V. |
| China monumentis | 1667 | Amsterdam | On the Far East. See our Chapter IV. |
| Magneticum naturae regnum | 1667 | Rome | A short work on magnetic experiments. |
| Ars magna sciendi | 1669 | Amsterdam | Kircher's system of logic, developed from the Art of Ramon Lull. |
| Splendor et gloria domus Joanniae, also titled Principis Christiani Archetypon Politicum | 1669 | Amsterdam | An encomium on the Imperial family. |
| Latium | 1671 | Amsterdam | On the region around Rome, ancient and modern. See our Chapter III. |
| Phonurgia nova | 1673 | Kempton | On acoustics. |
| Arca Noë | 1675 | Amsterdam | On Noah's Ark and the origins of languages. See our Chapter I. |
| Sphinx mystagoga | 1676 | Amsterdam | On mummies discovered in Memphis in 1672. |
| Tariffa Kircheriana | 1679 | Rome | A set of mathematical tables. |
| Turris Babel | 1679 | Amsterdam | On the Tower of Babel, ancient civilizations and languages. See our Chapter II. |
| Bonanni, Philippo, S. J., Rerum Naturalium Historia . . . in Museo Kircheriano | 1709 | Rome | Catalogue of the Kircher Museum. |
| Kestler, Johann Stephan, Physiologia Kircheriana Experimentalis | 1680 | Amsterdam | Summary of Kircher's experiments. |
| Mangetus, Johann Jacob, Bibliotheca Chemica Curiosa | 1702 | Geneva | Contains Kircher on alchemy and adds criticisms by others. |
| Schott, Caspar, Magia Universalis Naturae et Artis | 1657 | Würzburg and Bamberg | Includes much from Kircher's unpublished papers. |
| ——, Organum Mathematicum | 1660 | Würzburg | On a kind of primitive computer. |
| ——, Pantometrum Kircherianum | 1669 | Würzburg | On a 'geometric calculator'. |
| ——, Technica Curiosa | 1664 | Nuremberg | Scientific marvels, borrowed from Kircher. |
